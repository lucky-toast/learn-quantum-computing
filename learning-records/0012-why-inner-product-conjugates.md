# Sees why the inner product conjugates the first state

In chat session 6, the learner saw that the plain dot product fails for complex amplitudes: for S|1⟩ = (0, i) it gives i · i = −1 rather than a length of 1. They accepted conjugating the first state's entries as the fix. This updates LR-0011, which recorded the inner product for real amplitudes only. Bra notation ⟨ψ|φ⟩ was mentioned but not practiced, so do not count it as learned until Lesson 7 shows it.
