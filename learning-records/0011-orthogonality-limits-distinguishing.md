# Uses dot products to explain when states can be told apart

The learner computed |0⟩ · |+⟩ = 1/√2 and found that two marked states have a dot product of 0. Given that unitaries preserve dot products, they explained that no circuit can send |0⟩ and |+⟩ to |0⟩ and |1⟩, because the inputs are not already orthogonal. The dot product was new in this session and has only been taught for real amplitudes, so the complex case (with conjugation) still needs teaching before phase gates like S come back.
