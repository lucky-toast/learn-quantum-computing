# Designs entangling circuits by working backward

The learner solved all three Lesson 4 targets by undoing CNOT, reading which qubit's pairs held the amplitudes, and rejecting candidates because an all-zero pair cannot become nonzero. They also fluently wrote controlled gates as label maps. On the second target they switched from backward reasoning to forward trial and error after one step, so next practice should have them continue undoing gates all the way to |00⟩.

**Evidence:** Independently found X on B, H on A, CNOT, and H on A, Z on A, CNOT, with working notes.
