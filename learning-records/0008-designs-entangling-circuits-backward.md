# Designs entangling circuits by working backward

The learner solved all three Lesson 4 targets by undoing CNOT, reading which qubit's pairs held the amplitudes, and rejecting candidates because an all-zero pair cannot become nonzero. They also fluently wrote controlled gates as label maps. On the second target they kept reasoning backward: after undoing CNOT, they recognized that H was the gate that would take the state back, so they knew the state before it. Working backward is an established skill, so future practice should build on it rather than remediate it.

**Evidence:** Independently found X on B, H on A, CNOT, and H on A, Z on A, CNOT, with working notes.
