# Quantum Computing Glossary

Terms the learner has used correctly in this workspace. Amplitudes are written in the order (00, 01, 10, 11).

## Terms

**Inner product**:
Conjugate the first state's entries, multiply matching entries, and add: a*c + b*d. For real amplitudes the conjugates change nothing. In this workspace "dot product" is an accepted alias and always means this conjugated version.
_Avoid_: Overlap score, similarity

**Orthogonal**:
Describes two states whose inner product is 0. Only orthogonal states can be told apart with certainty by one measurement.
_Avoid_: Opposite, independent
