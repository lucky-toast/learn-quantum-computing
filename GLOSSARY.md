# Quantum Algorithms Glossary

Terms the learner has used correctly in this workspace. Amplitudes are written in the order (00, 01, 10, 11).

## Terms

**Dot product**:
The sum of the products of matching entries of two state vectors. Taught so far for real amplitudes only.
_Avoid_: Overlap score, similarity

**Orthogonal**:
Describes two states whose dot product is 0. Only orthogonal states can be told apart with certainty by one measurement.
_Avoid_: Opposite, independent
