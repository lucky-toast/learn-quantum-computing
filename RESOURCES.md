# Quantum Algorithms Resources

## Knowledge

- [Course: _Basics of Quantum Information_ by John Watrous (IBM Quantum Learning)](https://quantum.cloud.ibm.com/learning/en/courses/basics-of-quantum-information/single-systems/introduction)
  Rigorous, free foundation in states, measurements, and unitary operations. Use for: definitions and notation in every early lesson.
- [Course: _Fundamentals of Quantum Algorithms_ by John Watrous (IBM Quantum Learning)](https://eu-de.quantum.cloud.ibm.com/learning/courses/fundamentals-of-quantum-algorithms)
  Query algorithms, phase estimation, factoring, and search, with Qiskit implementations. Use for: studying design patterns after attempting problems independently.
- [Book: _Understanding Quantum Information and Computation_ by John Watrous](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.11536)
  Open PDF of the written course. Use for: offline reading and precise statements.
- [Essay: _Quantum Computing for the Very Curious_ by Andy Matuschak and Michael Nielsen](https://quantum.country/qcvc)
  Mechanics-first introduction with built-in spaced repetition. Use for: a second explanation and long-term retention of the basics.
- [Article: _Complex number_ (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_number)
  Polar form and the matrix view of complex numbers. Use for: why multiplication scales and rotates.
- [Article: _Euler's formula_ (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler%27s_formula)
  The link between exponentials and the unit circle. Use for: phase gates written as e^(iθ).

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Quantum Computing Stack Exchange](https://quantumcomputing.stackexchange.com/)
  Searchable expert Q&A. Use for: checking a design or asking why a circuit behaves as it does.
- [Qiskit community channels (via IBM support docs)](https://quantum.cloud.ibm.com/docs/support)
  Routes to Qiskit Slack and programming help. Use for: practical questions once lessons move to Python.
