Reading the | ⟩ notation

|name⟩ is a label for a column of amplitudes. The bar and angle bracket mark it as a quantum state, called a ket. The top entry is the amplitude for measuring 0, and the bottom entry is the amplitude for measuring 1.

Extending to more qubits

Each symbol inside the ket is one qubit, read left to right. So |01⟩ means qubit A is |0⟩ and qubit B is |1⟩. Combine them with the tensor product:

|01⟩ = |0⟩ ⊗ |1⟩ = 10 ⊗ 01 = 1·01·10·00·1 = 0100 ← 00← 01← 10← 11

The 1 lands in the "01" position. The same pattern covers any label: |+0⟩ = |+⟩ ⊗ |0⟩ = (1/√2, 0, 1/√2, 0), and |101⟩ is a column of 8 amplitudes with a 1 in the "101" position.

Design move

Put the answer into a sign, then interfere so it becomes a measurable outcome. Signs do not affect a direct measurement, but H turns them into cancellation or reinforcement.
