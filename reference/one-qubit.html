One qubit

State and measurement

|ψ⟩ = ab   with |a|² + |b|² = 1   P(0) = |a|², P(1) = |b|²

Named states

|0⟩ = 10   |1⟩ = 01   |+⟩ = 1/√21/√2   |−⟩ = 1/√2−1/√2

Gates

GateMatrixEffect
X0110Swaps the amplitudes
Z100−1Flips the sign of the bottom amplitude. Z|+⟩ = |−⟩, Z|−⟩ = |+⟩
H1/√2 111−1Top becomes sum, bottom becomes difference, scaled by 1/√2. H|0⟩ = |+⟩, H|1⟩ = |−⟩, H|+⟩ = |0⟩, H|−⟩ = |1⟩

Design move

Put the answer into a sign, then interfere so it becomes a measurable outcome. Signs do not affect a direct measurement, but H turns them into cancellation or reinforcement.
