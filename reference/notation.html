Notation

Each row gives the symbol, what it means in plain vectors and matrices, and an example.

States

SymbolMeaningExample
|ψ⟩, |φ⟩Ket: a state as a column. ψ is “psi”, φ is “phi”|+⟩ = (1/√2, 1/√2)
a|0⟩ + b|1⟩The column (a, b), written as a sum of basis kets|−⟩ = (|0⟩ − |1⟩)/√2
|ab⟩Two-qubit basis ket. A's digit on the left. Order 00, 01, 10, 11(a, b, c, d) = a|00⟩ + b|01⟩ + c|10⟩ + d|11⟩
z*Complex conjugate: flip the sign of the imaginary part. Also written z̄z* z = |z|²,   (2 − 3i)* = 2 + 3i
⟨ψ|Bra: the ket as a row, every entry conjugated. ⟨ψ| = |ψ⟩†|+i⟩ = (1/√2, i/√2) ⟹ ⟨+i| = (1/√2   −i/√2)
⟨ψ|φ⟩Inner product: bra times ket, a*c + b*d. 0 means orthogonal⟨ψ|ψ⟩ = 1,   ⟨+|+i⟩ = ½ + ½i
|⟨k|ψ⟩|²Probability of outcome k. ⟨k|ψ⟩ is the amplitude of k⟨0|ψ⟩ = a for |ψ⟩ = a|0⟩ + b|1⟩

Swapping the order conjugates the result: ⟨φ|ψ⟩ = ⟨ψ|φ⟩*.

Gates

SymbolMeaningExample
M†Dagger: swap rows with columns, then conjugate every entryS† = diag(1, −i) = S³,   H† = H
U†U = IU is unitary. Equivalently U⁻¹ = U†, so the dagger undoes the gateS†S = I
|φ⟩ ⊗ |ψ⟩Tensor product: (a, b) ⊗ (c, d) = (ac, ad, bc, bd). Also written |φ⟩|ψ⟩|0⟩ ⊗ |1⟩ = |01⟩
U ⊗ VU on A and V on B at once. I means do nothingH on A = H ⊗ I,   Z on B = I ⊗ Z

Circuits read left to right, but products read right to left. “H on A, then CNOT, then H on A” is (H ⊗ I) · CNOT · (H ⊗ I)|ψ⟩.

Phase

Global phase: c|ψ⟩ with |c| = 1, such as −|ψ⟩ or i|ψ⟩, is the same state as |ψ⟩. Relative phase: a factor on only some terms, such as |+⟩ versus |−⟩, changes the state.
